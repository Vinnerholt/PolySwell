PolySwell II - installing on a Mac
==================================

Easiest: double-click "install-mac.command".
If macOS says it cannot be opened: right-click it > Open > Open. On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
click Done, then System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
It copies the plugins to your user plug-in folders and clears the "downloaded from the internet" flag.

By hand instead:
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy "PolySwell II.vst3"       to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   Copy "PolySwell II.component"  to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (Logic, GarageBand)
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder... and paste the path.)
3. PolySwell II is free and not signed with a paid Apple developer account, so macOS blocks it at first.
   Open Terminal and paste:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/PolySwell\ II.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/PolySwell\ II.component
4. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. Logic: restart it; it validates the Audio Unit on start.

Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.13 or newer).
