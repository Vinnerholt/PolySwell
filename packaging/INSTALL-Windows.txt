PolySwell II - installing on Windows
====================================

1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the folder "PolySwell II.vst3" into
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows asks for administrator permission; that is normal.)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it does not find it by itself.
   It shows up as "PolySwell II" by Benno (an effect, VST3).

Presets you save go to Documents\PolySwell\Presets.
